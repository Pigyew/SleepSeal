Open SleepSeal

Open SleepSeal from Applications if its setup window did not appear. The first-run guide asks you to confirm protection and Bluetooth permission. Existing users keep their previous protection behavior after a safe migration.

If macOS blocks this unsigned Beta, review System Settings → Privacy & Security and approve SleepSeal after checking its published checksum and source. Do not disable system security.

Power timer changes that you apply in SleepSeal are macOS settings and remain in effect when SleepSeal exits. Apple silicon deep sleep remains managed by macOS.
