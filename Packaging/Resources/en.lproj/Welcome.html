SleepSeal 0.2.1 Beta

SleepSeal helps reduce unwanted wireless and power activity while your MacBook sleeps. Choose the sleep events you want to protect using the native dashboard.

This installer adds SleepSeal to Applications and installs a limited, root-owned power-setting tool. macOS asks for an administrator account through its normal Installer interface.

This Beta uses a temporary ad-hoc signature and has not been notarized. After installation, open SleepSeal and follow the macOS approval and Bluetooth permission instructions. Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later. Sleep behavior and battery savings vary by Mac and software.
